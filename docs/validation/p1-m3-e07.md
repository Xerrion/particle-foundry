## Hardware ABI sentinel

[`GpuCellHeader` and `GpuGridParams`](../../engine/crates/sim/src/gpu_layout.rs)
pack fields explicitly in little-endian form. The
[Rust GPU validator](../../engine/crates/sim-gpu/src/abi.rs) uploads Rust-packed
grid, cell and component-major mass data. Its
[WGSL shader](../../engine/crates/sim-gpu/shaders/abi_sentinel.wgsl) reads the
first and last cells and both mass slots. Rust compares all 12 returned u32
words with independently calculated values. The fixture uses 208 bytes across
its uniform, input, result and staging buffers; only 48 bytes are read back.

The same device checks the lower-bound storage plan for a 480 x 270 grid with
two active components before the sentinel allocates buffers. This core-field
plan is 3,113,416 bytes. It excludes pressure/transport scratch, rendering,
other staging, and driver allocations, so passing preflight does not establish
that a complete scene can allocate or run. The current GPU grid uniform rejects
nondefault cell spacing.

## Coupled stage graph and small-scene checks

Native small-grid pressure, phase/marker transport, compatible momentum and
tangential shear fixtures pass as separate stage-level work. Independent review
found a near-uniform
phase-interface branch mismatch. The transport shader now uses the CPU
reference threshold, and the added native GPU regression reports maximum
liquid-fraction error `4.098e-8` against the frozen `1e-5` gate. Native
momentum parity on a nonuniform 2 x 2 vortex is within `2e-5` m/s, with
shared transverse flux records and wall-impulse agreement within
`2e-11` kg m/s. Native 4 x 4 shear parity covers variable density and
viscosity, fractional/blocked apertures, and invalid bound rejection.

The [private coupled scene](../../engine/crates/sim-gpu/src/scene/coupled.rs)
owns one committed generation and detached candidate buffers. It runs GPU CFL,
phase/marker transport, compatible momentum, derived density, optional shear,
gravity and pressure in order. Each candidate checks five 4-byte stage status
words, and pressure checks true residual and corrected divergence through a
compact reduction. Only a successful projection copies inventory into the
alternate committed generation and advances model time. Normal stepping does
not download the full scene. Qualification fixtures read small-scene fields as
parity and rollback oracles only.

The native 2 x 2 mixed water/air vortex passes one accepted outer tick against
the E06 CPU reference at equal accepted time, with liquid-fraction mean absolute
error at most `1e-5` and scaled velocity RMS at most `1e-4`. The fixture uses
both phases, nonunit carrier density and viscosity. A separate zero-force
fixture completes a tick. After one accepted mixed-phase tick switches the
committed generation, a forced pressure failure leaves its mass, marker,
density and both velocity buffers byte-identical. The generation, epoch, tick,
accepted and encoded time, and remaining outer time also stay unchanged.
Test-only fault injection covers all five stage gates and pressure. Retrying
after rejection matches a clean second step byte for byte. The clock test
completes 1,000 nominal outer ticks while accounting for the f32 shader step
representation. The clock divides an outer interval into balanced substeps
when a stability-limited step would otherwise leave a tiny final interval.
GPU status and CFL map operations use fresh staging buffers, including after an
abandoned asynchronous map.

The [browser bridge](../../engine/crates/wasm/src/lib.rs) exposes a compact
qualification report from an owned asynchronous Promise. The required-GPU
[browser smoke](../../web/tests/browser/engine-smoke.ts) passed on Chrome
`BrowserWebGpu`: epoch 2, tick 8, one accepted substep, accepted time
`0.13333333333333333` s, scaled residual `3.3113696e-11`, scaled divergence
`0`, and encoded-time error `-8.6923440667519e-10` s. A rejected
candidate preserved committed GPU fields and clock state. Both ABI and scene
validations completed cleanly when the Engine was disposed during the Promise;
the operations kept their cloned GPU handles until settlement. The current
required-GPU browser result is
`artifacts/validation/e08/browser-sustained-balanced-2026-09-29/result.json`.
Offline WASM build/freshness, target check and Clippy, TypeScript, Biome and
formatting passed for this slice. Full repository CI remains pending.

## Full-size numerical checks and delivery gates

The pressure stage uses reduced f32 PCG with a bounded component-mean gauge.
The scene owner builds static component roots from validated wall and aperture
topology. A GPU list groups cells by root without a floating-point atomic sum;
the gauge subtracts each component's mean. Manufactured, disconnected and
nonfinite-predictor small fixtures pass. The sealed 480 x 270 smooth-flow
fixture now meets the frozen `1e-5` gates in 12 iterations on native Metal:
scaled true residual `8.011991e-6`, corrected divergence `8.012359e-6`,
and about 80.95 ms inside the projection call in that run. No tolerance or
iteration cap was relaxed. Those numbers measure the isolated pressure stage.
The initial browser qualification fixture checked a small coupled scene and
its diagnostics. The field-by-field CPU/GPU parity oracle ran natively. The
[E08 evidence](p1-m3-e08.md) records the persistent browser scene, same-device
rendering, bounded control path and sustained Chrome run.

The full-size scene exposed f32 cancellation between its hydrostatic pressure
and small dynamic correction. The pressure stage now keeps those terms
separate. A native 4 x 3 fixture with horizontally varying density and gravity
passed in eight iterations, with scaled true residual `3.272163e-6` and
corrected divergence `3.2852406e-6`. All nine native pressure tests pass.
An internal PCG stop margin is half of the frozen final thresholds. The final
`1e-5` residual and divergence gates and 512-iteration cap remain unchanged.

The transport stage uses a corrected shared-face ledger and a per-donor budget
for two outlets. Its Y budget uses inventory after the X transfer. Focused
native regressions cover that ordering and local closure. With this transport
path, the pressure split and balanced clock substeps, the 480 x 270 native Metal
scene completed 120 outer ticks in 147 accepted substeps over 2.0 model
seconds. Its maximum recorded pressure residual was `4.9995274e-6`; its
maximum corrected divergence was `5.0218528e-6`. See the
[E08 evidence](p1-m3-e08.md) for the scene and browser scope.

The committed native regression uses 60 ticks. It passed again on Metal after
the implementation commit with
`cd engine && cargo test --locked -p particle-sim-gpu scene::demo::tests::native_metal_full_scene_tick_and_committed_render -- --ignored --exact --nocapture`.
Its ignored local log is
`artifacts/validation/e07/native-demo-committed-2026-09-29.log`. The 120-tick
trial changed only this test's temporary loop bound to extend the observation;
the committed production solver is the same. The required-GPU 60-tick Chrome
command and measured result are in [E08 evidence](p1-m3-e08.md).
